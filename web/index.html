Where this could go

  • Add a sweat-cortisol channel. Skin conductance, which tracks sweat gland activity, is the strongest single signal here. A wearable cortisol patch would add a direct hormonal measure of stress alongside it.
  • Track recovery after concussion. The same pipeline could follow stress responses across a patient's recovery, compared with that patient's own baseline.
  • Account for hormone cycles. Stress and cortisol responses shift across the menstrual cycle. A cycle-aware baseline could reduce false alarms for female wearers.

Data: Stress-Predict dataset (Iqbal et al., 2022; MIT licence), 35 volunteers wearing an Empatica E4 wristband during a Stroop test, an interview and hyperventilation. One participant with irregular task markers is excluded, as in the original study. Research prototype, not a medical device.
